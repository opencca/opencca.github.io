What is OpenCCA?

OpenCCA is an open research framework designed to enable early exploration and evaluation of Arm Confidential Compute Architecture (CCA) on vendor lock-free non-CCA hardware.

This makes it possible to estimate the performance of Arm CCA research designs and interact with real-world peripherals, going beyond the capabilities of pure software simulation.
